The story

How AI is changing the way we work

Four steps, from today's manual work to systems that think alongside your team.

01

Work is changing

AI is taking over routine work such as reading documents, sorting requests, drafting replies and spotting patterns. People move to the parts that need judgement.

02

Data is the fuel

AI is only as good as the data beneath it. Clean, connected and well-governed data comes first. Moving old systems to the cloud is one step on that road, not the destination.

03

AI inside real workflows

The value is in connecting AI to the systems people already use, with testing, measurement and human review, not in a demo that sits on its own.

04

Engineers make it trustworthy

Someone must design the architecture, check the results, secure the data and be accountable when something goes wrong. Software engineers turn AI into a dependable system.

That is the work we do.

We build and run the data, AI and software behind every step above.

Questions

Straight answers

Who owns the code?

Custom work delivered to you belongs to you once it is paid for, as set out in the written agreement. We state any open-source or pre-existing components up front.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign yours before you share any detail, or to provide ours.

How is pricing set?

Fixed price for well-defined scope, with payments tied to milestones. Time-and-material for team augmentation. Estimates are written and any change is agreed before it affects cost.

Which time zones do you work in?

We agree working hours that overlap with yours for meetings and decisions, and communicate in clear written English.

How do we start?

A free 30-minute call, then usually a small fixed-scope first piece such as an audit or pilot, so you can judge our work before committing more.
